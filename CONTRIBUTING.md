# Contributing to AKILTA Core

AKILTA Core is a private proprietary engineering repository. Contributions are
accepted only from contributors and agents who are authorized to work on this
project.

This file supplements the repository engineering rules in `AGENTS.md`. It does
not replace any employment, contractor, invention-assignment, confidentiality,
or other binding agreement.

## Rights and provenance

By submitting material to this repository, a contributor must have the right to
submit it for the authorized AKILTA project use.

Do not submit:

- code, assets, models, datasets, prompts, documentation, or other material taken
  from a third party without a usable rights basis;
- confidential, leaked, stolen, or access-controlled material;
- code copied from a source merely because it is publicly viewable;
- material whose license obligations are unknown or incompatible with the
  intended AKILTA use;
- real secrets, credentials, customer data, or private keys.

Third-party material that is intentionally incorporated must preserve its
license, copyright, attribution, provenance, modification/use record, and any
other applicable obligations. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the central
AKILTA IP/licensing policy.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted output is not automatically provenance-free. Contributors and agents
must not use AI as a laundering mechanism for proprietary, confidential, or
unlicensed third-party material.

If AI output appears to reproduce identifiable third-party material, treat it as
third-party material until its rights basis is resolved.

## Ownership

Repository access, commit authorship, AI generation, payment, or possession does
not by itself determine legal ownership.

Ownership of contributed material follows the applicable AKILTA
company/project records and any employment, contractor, assignment, or license
agreement governing that contribution.

## Engineering process

Contributions must follow `AGENTS.md`, the active task/execution contract, and
the current repository authority. Do not use a legal/IP cleanup as authority to
change product scope, architecture, current engineering selectors, or release
status.

## Security

Follow `SECURITY.md`. Never commit real secrets or customer-sensitive data.
