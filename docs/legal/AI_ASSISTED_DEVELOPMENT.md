# AI-Assisted Development Policy

This repository's engineering work is performed with the assistance of AI
coding agents (see `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` for the operating contract
governing that work). This document records the policy for how that
assistance is attributed and reviewed, for provenance purposes.

## Attribution and authorship

- Decisions about what gets built, what scope is authorized, and what is
  accepted into this repository are made by the project's human owner
  (Founder) and, within the authorized engineering corridor, by the
  ChatGPT/"Brain" verification role described in `AGENTS.md`. AI-assisted
  engineering execution (Claude, acting as "Primary Engineer" per
  `CLAUDE.md`) implements within that authorized scope; it does not set
  scope, strategy, or legal position on its own.
- These decisions and their provenance are attributable through this
  project's own records: git commit history and authorship metadata,
  pull request descriptions, and the execution-plan documents under
  `docs/exec-plans/`.
- AI participation in producing source code, tests, or documentation in
  this repository is not represented, and must not be represented
  elsewhere, as independent legal authorship separate from the human
  ownership and decision-making described above. Where attribution
  metadata (for example, commit trailers) names an AI assistant, that
  metadata records which tool performed the mechanical work — it is not
  a claim of independent legal authorship or ownership by that tool.

## Provenance review for generated or imported material

- Source code, text, or other material generated by an AI assistant for
  this repository is treated the same as any other first-party
  contribution for ownership purposes (see `NOTICE.md`), subject to the
  human review and acceptance process described in `AGENTS.md`.
- Any asset that is imported from, or generated with reference to, an
  external source (for example, a third-party library, a dataset, or a
  pre-existing design asset) requires a provenance and license review
  before being accepted, and should be recorded in
  `docs/legal/IP_PROVENANCE.md` and, if it is a dependency,
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Where the provenance of a generated or imported asset cannot currently
  be verified, that fact is recorded explicitly (`TBD` / `UNKNOWN`) in
  `docs/legal/IP_PROVENANCE.md` rather than assumed to be clear.

## Evidence preservation

- Task execution records under `docs/exec-plans/` and the corresponding
  git/PR history are preserved as the evidence trail for how AI-assisted
  work was scoped, implemented, and reviewed.
- This policy does not require, and this repository does not publish,
  the internal prompts or conversational context used to produce a given
  change merely for the sake of provenance record-keeping. Any such
  material that must be shared for a specific verification purpose is
  reviewed for secrets and sensitive content first, per `AGENTS.md` §5
  (secrets fail closed).

## Not legal advice

This document describes engineering process and record-keeping. It is
not a legal opinion on the copyrightability, ownership, or enforceability
of any AI-assisted output, and it does not substitute for legal review
where that is required.
