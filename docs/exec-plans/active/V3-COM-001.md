## Architecture / semantic invariants

- `commissionLedgerStatus` is a single-literal type — structurally incapable of ever claiming a known commission ledger state, since no commission/billing/settlement system exists anywhere in this repository.
- `discountAuthorityLevel` is always `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`: absent a real, authoritative DEC-146 pricing-policy integration (none exists in this repo), no role or ownership label can silently grant a more permissive level — this is the fail-closed default, not an invented financial value.
- `buildCommercialAuthoritySnapshot` does not import `outcome-job.ts` at all, so delivery/job completion state cannot influence commission ledger status even by accident (§8: "delivery completion never implies commission settlement/payment").
- `dealOwnerMembershipId` reuses `resolveCurrentOwner` (V3-OWN-001) verbatim for the `DEAL_OWNER` role; a history entry for a foreign tenant/customer/project is never matched (delegated entirely to `resolveCurrentOwner`'s own exact-scope filtering) — a caller cannot smuggle in an unrelated deal's owner.
- Zero numeric (`: number`) field exists on `CommercialAuthoritySnapshot` — structurally cannot carry a commission percentage, discount amount, or currency value.
- No import of `authority.ts` (cannot grant permission), `partner-organization.ts` (commercial visibility cannot be derived through a partner/agency identity), or anything from `src/web/`/`src/application/`.

## Hard Non-Scope

No commission percentage/basis/attribution/reversal/tax/payout; no discount threshold/limit/coupon logic; no reseller/wholesale/co-brand economics; no binding SLA term; no payment/settlement/invoice/refund action; no persistence/database; no HTTP/UI surface; no AI Commerce mutation; no merge/deploy/release/production/publication/customer-binding/legal/financial commitment anywhere in this checkpoint's source.
